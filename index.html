My Journey

From Curiosity to Code

My journey into Computer Science began with a simple but powerful feeling — curiosity. From the moment I first interacted with technology, I knew I wanted to understand how it works and, eventually, build things with it. That curiosity turned into passion, and passion led me to pursue a BTech in Computer Science Engineering.

I am currently specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity at Newton School of Technology. These two fields excite me the most — AI because of its limitless potential to solve complex problems, and Cybersecurity because of the critical role it plays in protecting the digital world.

I am still early in my journey, actively learning programming, exploring core concepts, and working on small projects to build a strong foundation. My long-term goal is to become a skilled software developer, contribute to meaningful technology, and grow both as an engineer and as a professional.
